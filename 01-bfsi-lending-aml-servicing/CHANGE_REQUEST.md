# Business Change Request

Business wants to reduce manual fraud/AML triage and loan-servicing exceptions while preserving existing lending and payment behavior. A proposed enhancement should surface high-risk cases with evidence, explain routing, support analyst review, and avoid autonomous financial decisions unless explicitly approved.

## Workshop expectation

Treat this as a business pressure statement, not an approved solution design. Use the production-delivery spine to establish the baseline, qualify AI-vs-no-AI, define specifications, preserve critical existing behavior, and prove measurable value.
