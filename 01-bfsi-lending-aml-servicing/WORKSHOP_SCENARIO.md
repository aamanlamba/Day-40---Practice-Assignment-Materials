# Workshop Scenario

**Domain:** BFSI — Digital Lending, AML & Loan Servicing

You have inherited this repository during an enterprise transformation. Business users depend on it, but documentation and ownership are incomplete. Different teams have introduced newer APIs and frontend code while older batch jobs, adapters and rules remain active. The synthetic data mimics operational diversity, inconsistent classifications, missingness and edge cases.

## Mission

Apply the complete AI FDE production-delivery spine: establish facts and boundaries, baseline behavior and KPIs, qualify AI-vs-no-AI, create PRDs/specifications/traceability, modernize safely, engineer any justified intelligence/agentic capabilities, validate through TEVV, harden, release, instrument, measure economics/value, define ownership and produce an authoritative final as-built PRD.

## Non-negotiables

- This is brownfield, not greenfield.
- Preserve and prove critical behavior before intentional change.
- Evidence outranks assumptions.
- High-impact actions remain bounded by explicit human/deterministic controls.
- Artifacts generated by the delivery spine belong under `docs/` using the agreed stage structure; inherited documents remain under `docs/legacy/`.
