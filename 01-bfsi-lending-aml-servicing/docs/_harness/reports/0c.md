# Stage 0C Report — Provisional Token Efficiency & AI Economics Envelope

## 1. Stage Status

PASS. All 13 artifacts exist under `docs/00-preflight/ai-economics/`. The envelope is provisional and does not justify AI; AI-specific parts are contingent on Stage 8.

## 2. Key Findings

- Measured baseline (`data/baseline_metrics.csv`, 180 days): 127,617 work items (about 709/day), 8,273.5 manual hours (about 3.89 min/item), 4.80% error rate, 7.29% exception rate, 2.90 cost units/item.
- The repo has no model calls today; `experimental_ai.py` is a keyword counter and is not called.
- Whole-entity datasets are 200k+ tokens each, so any AI design must retrieve per case. Expected single-shot case is about 1.7k input / 0.3k output tokens; agentic is about 15k / 1.25k.
- At assumed mid-tier prices, expected-volume token cost is about $497/year (single-shot) and $3,299/year (agentic). People and evaluation effort dominate in the placeholder TCO.
- Provisional constraints: $0.10 marginal AI cost per case, p95 latency 5 s (single-shot) / 30 s (agentic), 4k/8k context, 6 loop steps, 8 calls.

## 3. Major Risks

- Unit prices, labour rates and effort weeks are assumptions, so all cost figures carry large uncertainty.
- The meaning of "work item" and "cost unit" is unknown, so ratios against the baseline may mislead.
- The volume slice AI could touch is unknown (52 to 709 cases/day).
- No API latency data exists to anchor latency limits.
- Figures could be mistaken for a business case.

## 4. Assumptions / Unknowns

Assumptions: prices (small/mid/large tiers), $30/h analyst cost, $1,500/person-week, 20% expected volume share, chars/4 tokenization, effort weeks. Unknowns: work-item and cost-unit definitions, alert arrival rate, real vendor pricing, business budget and value targets.
